# Getting Started with Cybersecurity in 2025

Cybersecurity is more critical than ever in a world increasingly dependent on digital infrastructure. This guide will help you understand the basics of cybersecurity, the current threat landscape, and how to get started.

## What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. These cyberattacks are typically aimed at accessing, changing, or destroying sensitive information; extorting money; or disrupting business operations.

## Key Concepts

- **CIA Triad**: Core principles—Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability
- **Threats and Vulnerabilities**: Understanding common attack vectors and security flaws
- **Authentication and Authorization**: Controlling access to resources
- **Defense in Depth**: Layered security strategy to mitigate risks

## Common Threats in 2025

- **AI-Enhanced Phishing**: Sophisticated phishing campaigns using generative AI
- **Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS)**: Organized cybercrime selling attack tools
- **IoT Exploits**: Attacks on smart devices due to poor default security
- **Supply Chain Attacks**: Targeting software dependencies and third-party vendors
