# Introduction to Ethical Hacking in 2025

Cyber attacks are increasing both in number and complexity, so organizations need ethical hackers who can timely discover and fix security vulnerabilities before attacks happen.

## What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical hacking, also known as penetration testing or white-hat hacking, involves legally accessing systems to identify security vulnerabilities. Ethical hackers act like attackers to improve defense mechanisms.

## Types of Hackers

- **White Hat**: Authorized ethical hackers  
- **Black Hat**: Malicious attackers  
- **Gray Hat**: Operate without permission but without malicious intent  

## Phases of Ethical Hacking

1. **Reconnaissance**: Gathering information about the target  
2. **Scanning**: Searching for live hosts, open ports, and vulnerabilities  
3. **Gaining Access**: Exploiting vulnerabilities to enter the system  
4. **Maintaining Access**: Simulating persistent threats  
5. **Covering Tracks**: (Simulated) erasing traces to avoid detection  

## How to Get Started

### Recommended Skills:
- Basic networking principles (TCP/IP, ports, protocols)  
- Basic knowledge of Linux and scripting (Bash, Python)  
- Familiarity with OWASP Top 10  

### Tools to Learn:
- **Kali Linux**  
- **Nmap**  
- **Hydra**  
- **Burp Suite**  
- **SQLMap**  

### Learning Platforms:
- [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/)  
- [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com/)  
- [PentesterLab](https://pentesterlab.com/)  

### Certifications:
- **CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)**  
- **eJPT (Junior Penetration Tester)**  
- **OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional)**  

Ethical hacking is not just about using tools — it's a learning approach, curiosity, and persistence. Practice legally, be ethical, and contribute to a safer internet.
